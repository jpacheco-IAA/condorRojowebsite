03 / THE RED PLANET

Mars is full of surprises.

Six facts that make Mars an incredible place to explore.

−63°C

Average temperature

Mars is extremely cold, with temperatures that vary greatly.

38%

Earth's gravity

Gravity on Mars is about 38% of Earth's.

687

Days in a Mars year

One orbit around the Sun takes 687 Earth days.

24h 39m

One Martian sol

A Martian solar day is a little longer than an Earth day.

~95%

Carbon dioxide

Most of Mars's thin atmosphere is carbon dioxide.

02

Natural moons
