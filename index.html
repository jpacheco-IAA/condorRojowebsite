06 / RADIATION & SHIELDING

A solar storm changes everything.

In Cóndor Rojo, warnings and shelter decisions help you navigate radiation hazards. This mechanic draws on NASA observations of space weather and Mars radiation.

01 / GCR

Cosmic rays are persistent

High-energy galactic cosmic rays create ongoing exposure. They are especially difficult to block completely.

02 / SPE

Solar events can raise the risk

Energetic particles from the Sun can cause sudden increases in exposure. Shelter and early warnings matter.

03 / SHIELD

Protection involves trade-offs

Water and polyethylene are among the materials NASA studies for shielding. No practical shield eliminates all space radiation.

IN CÓNDOR ROJO · FEATURE IN DEVELOPMENT

We're exploring a portable shield-capacity mechanic: plan a trip, use limited protection, and return to the habitat to replenish supplies. Shield capacity can be restored; accumulated radiation dose cannot. This is a simplified fictional mechanic inspired by real research, not a real rechargeable radiation field.
